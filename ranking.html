The Dell Optiplex 7060 Micro is an unassuming machine. It is incredibly tiny and seems like it would lack the computing power necessary to do much of anything. After being donated 3 of these at robotics, I initially thought the best use we'd have for them is to use them around the room to display various webpages we use in a permanent place. However after some Googling, I learned the shocking truth, these are actually considerably powerful for their size. We were able to run solidworks (in an admittedly limited fashion) on these tiny machines. The pure surprise of their power really surprised me, but for their price point there are definitely better options out there.
