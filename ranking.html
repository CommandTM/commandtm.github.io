The Big Ranking Of THINGS

This is my big ranking of random things. Anything is eligible for this list. Things closer to the top are the best while things closer to the bottom are worse. Expand any given item to read more about my opinion.

Othello is a game I was aware of since the beginning of time but only became familiar with how to play it in 2023. For learning 2D arrays in programming class, we were tasked with programming a console Othello game. After a few days of writing terrible code because I barely knew what I was doing, I had finished this assignment. Throughout the time that people in the class were coding the Othello project (it quickly became infamous in the class) you could ask someone to play Othello, and they'd most likely be down. The best part was that you could pass off your Othello game was work because you were studying how the game works or teaching someone who was unsure. Besides these memories, Othello is simply a fun game to play. It has a deceptive amount of strategy and makes for a lot of fun times.

The Dell Optiplex 7060 Micro is an unassuming machine. It is incredibly tiny and seems like it would lack the computing power necessary to do much of anything. After being donated 3 of these at robotics, I initially thought the best use we'd have for them is to use them around the room to display various webpages we use in a permanent place. However after some Googling, I learned the shocking truth, these are actually considerably powerful for their size. We were able to run solidworks (in an admittedly limited fashion) on these tiny machines. The pure surprise of their power really surprised me, but for their price point there are definitely better options out there.

After I crashed my 2011 Chevy Equinox I had to downgrade by 4 years for a week while I was getting a new car. The particular 2007 Chevy Equinox I was driving had upwards of 200,000 miles on it, leaked oil, didn't have a functioning fuel gage, and had a horrible stereo. When I got a new car I was very happy to stop driving this one.

JavaScript feels like it was a programming language made in 10 days that shouldn't have gone farther than its own creation. It feels like this because it is this, it WAS made in 10 days and shouldn't have gotten to where it is now. It's a miserable language to write anything in and once you finally get something working it reads like ancient runes on a stone tablet.
